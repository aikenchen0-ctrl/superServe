2026-11-09 follow-up:
The native/android RustTimeLoadControl consumer uses the public LoadControl API
of the published media3-exoplayer/common 1.11.1 artifacts. It delegates allocator,
startup, preloading and lifecycle behavior to the original LoadControl.
Only the existing Rust time hysteresis is wrapped through UniFFI; no copy of
DefaultLoadControl or ExoPlayerImplInternal was added to the consumer.
The published source JAR is recorded separately from the research commit above.
