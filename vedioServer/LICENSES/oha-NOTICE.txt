examples/support/load_schedule.rs adapts the fixed-rate absolute arrival
schedule from src/client.rs work_with_qps_latency_correction and scheduled
request timing from RequestResult::duration. It does not copy the HTTP engine,
TUI, unbounded kanal queue, parser, histograms, or complete CLI.

Changes: bounded JoinSet/Semaphore admission, explicit dropped arrivals,
planned-deadline timing including scheduler lag, raw outcome-preserving samples,
and exact nearest-rank percentiles for a bounded experiment. No implicit retry,
unbounded wait queue, or coordinated-omission correction through resampling.
The profile protocol checks and process harness are local domain integration.
